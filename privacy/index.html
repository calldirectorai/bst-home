Privacy Policy.

Last updated: October 14, 2026

Bright Seal Tax ("we", "us") provides tax preparation, credit coaching and mobile notary services. This policy explains what information we collect through this website and by phone or text, and how we use it.

Information we collect

When you submit our request form or contact us, we collect the details you give us: your name, phone number, email address, the service you need, your preferred time to be contacted and any message you write. We also receive basic technical data such as your browser type and pages visited.

What we ask you not to send

Please do not send Social Security numbers, bank account details or tax documents through this website, by text or by email. We will show you how to share documents securely once you are a client.

How we use your information

We use your information to respond to your request, schedule your consultation, provide the services you ask for and follow up about your request. If you agreed to receive text messages, we use your mobile number to text you about that request.

Text messaging

Text messages are optional and sent only if you check the consent box on our form. No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties. You can reply STOP at any time to opt out. See our SMS Terms.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers that help us run our website, phone and messaging systems, and only as needed to provide those services, or when the law requires it.

Security and retention

We use reasonable safeguards to protect your information and keep it only as long as needed to serve you and meet legal and tax-record obligations.

Your choices

You can ask us to update or delete the contact information you gave us, or stop contacting you, by emailing yolandeosab@brightsealtax.com or calling (888) 561-7443.
