Tax planning looks ahead.

Tax planning means reviewing your financial situation and understanding how your income, expenses, life changes and decisions may affect your taxes, before you file.

Get my free Tax Planning Check-Up

Did any of this happen to you this year?

Each of these changes may affect your tax return.

Did your income increase?

A raise or bonus can change your bracket and your withholding.

Did you start a business?

New income, new expenses and new deductions to plan for.

Did you get married?

Your filing status may change, and so can your tax picture.

Did you have a child?

A new dependent can open up family and dependent credits.

Did you start working independently?

1099 and self-employed work usually means planning for estimated taxes.

Did you receive additional income?

Side income, investments or rental income all count.

Preparation vs. planning

Tax preparation

Looks at the information needed to complete your return.

Tax planning

Looks ahead and helps you make informed decisions before you file.
